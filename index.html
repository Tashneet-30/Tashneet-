About

Hey, I'm Tashneet! ⚡🚌

I'm a Master of Applied Computing (Thesis) student at Wilfrid Laurier University in Waterloo — building at the intersection of artificial intelligence, transportation, and smart urban mobility.

My path hasn't been a straight line, and I think that's what makes the work interesting. I started in cybersecurity — monitoring threats, responding to incidents, and managing client operations — then moved into open-source development, published research on transit systems, and now lead the machine learning side of HIEV-AI, an industry collaboration with Elocity Technologies. What ties it all together is a fascination with systems that have to work in the real world — whether that's securing a network, shipping code with a global team, or helping a condo building figure out where its EV chargers should go.

I care about work where algorithms meet actual cities: predicting how people move, planning infrastructure before it's too late, and building software that helps communities transition to cleaner mobility. I also believe technology works best when paired with clear communication — something I practice as a teaching assistant, a mentor, and a content creator.

Things I've worked on

  • ⚡HIEV-AI — the machine learning developer on an OCI-funded platform with Elocity Technologies and Laurier, building AI tools that help property developers plan EV charging in multi-unit residential buildings.
  • 🚆Digital twin–based passenger demand forecasting for public transit — manuscript accepted in Future Generation Computer Systems (Elsevier).
  • 🚨WayWise — a real-time AI system for emergency vehicle routing and smart city traffic analysis.
  • 🌐Open-source developer at the Khalis Foundation, shipping AI-powered features to the Hazur app with an international team through pull requests and collaborative development.
  • 🔐SOC Analyst at SafeAeon Inc. — monitoring threats with Rapid7 SIEM and SentinelOne, investigating incidents, and learning how to think under pressure in fast-moving security environments.
  • 📋Service Delivery Manager at SafeAeon — leading client communication, SLA operations, and cross-functional technical teams before moving into research.
  • 📚Graduate Teaching Assistant at Laurier (CP363 & CP373) — helping students with programming, SQL, and databases while pursuing my own thesis.
  • 🤝Graduate International Student Leader at Laurier International — mentoring international students and building inclusive campus communities.
